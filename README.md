Extract real-time startup jobs, founder direct contact emails, salary ranges, and remote policies directly from the monthly official **Hacker News "Who is Hiring?"** threads.

Powered by the official Hacker News Firebase REST API: Ultra-fast HTTP, zero browser overhead, zero proxy fees.

---

### Features
- **Direct Founder & Recruiter Emails:** Instant extraction of hiring contact emails.
- **Structured Fields:** Company name, role, location, remote status, application link, and full posting text.
- **Real-Time Sync:** Automatically finds and scrapes the latest active monthly thread.
- **Pay-Per-Event:** Only $0.003 per extracted lead. Zero compute or proxy surcharges.

---

### Integrations
- **Google Sheets & Airtable:** Export leads for sales and candidate outreach.
- **Make & Zapier:** Trigger automated email sequences when a matching company hires.
- **n8n:** Custom lead enrichment pipelines.
